Privacy Policy · Last updated February 23, 2026

TL;DR

FocusMind stores everything on your device. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information. No accounts required.

Data stored on your device

All your data — tasks, habits, medication logs, journal entries, focus sessions, and notes — lives on your iPhone only using SwiftData. It never leaves your device. We cannot access it.

Subscription data

Apple processes all payments through the App Store. We never see your payment information — only a confirmation that your subscription is active.

What we do NOT collect

  • ❌ No names, emails, or accounts
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No microphone or camera access
  • ❌ No analytics or usage tracking
  • ❌ No advertising identifiers
  • ❌ No data shared with third parties

Crash reports

If the app crashes, Apple may collect anonymized diagnostic data per standard iOS behavior. You control this in Settings → Privacy → Analytics & Improvements.

Children's privacy

FocusMind is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your data, your control

Since all data is on your device: view it by opening the app, delete it by deleting the app.
